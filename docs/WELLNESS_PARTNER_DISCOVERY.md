# ResoFit Wellness Partner Discovery & Outreach

## Purpose

Build a continuously expanding directory of spas, wellness centres, physiotherapy providers, recovery studios, hotels with spa services, and related wellness businesses across Nigeria and Africa.

## Source strategy

Use public business/directory sources and official business websites as discovery inputs. Current discovery sources include:

- Go Africa Online — Nigeria and other African markets
- Fresha — Nigeria spa and wellness listings
- Finelib — Nigeria spa listings
- BusinessList Nigeria — health spa listings
- Tripadvisor — Nigeria spa and wellness listings
- Spa Directory — African/South African spa directory
- Official business websites and publicly listed contact pages

Every imported record must retain its source URL and source name. Discovery does not equal ResoFit verification.

## Data lifecycle

`discovered → deduplicated → review → verified → ChatB2K searchable`

A business may be searchable as an **external prospect** before verification, but ChatB2K must never label it a ResoFit Verified Wellness Hub until the verification status is explicitly changed to `verified`.

## Partner outreach

For records with a public email or official website, ChatB2K can create a partnership outreach **draft**. Draft creation is automatic; sending is intentionally not automatic. A human must approve an outreach record before any message is sent.

Recommended partnership categories:

- Wellness directory listing
- Referral partnership
- Hotel/spa integration
- Corporate wellness
- ResoFit/ResoFlex brand collaboration
- Cross-promotion
- Practitioner/service partnership
- Affiliate or booking partnership

## Safety and quality rules

1. Never fabricate a contact email, website, address, rating, or verification status.
2. Prefer official websites for outreach contacts when available.
3. Preserve the source URL for every discovery record.
4. Deduplicate by business name + city + country, then review possible matches manually.
5. Respect public-site terms, robots rules, rate limits, and applicable privacy/marketing laws.
6. Do not automatically send bulk unsolicited outreach.
7. Store outreach as drafts until explicitly approved.
